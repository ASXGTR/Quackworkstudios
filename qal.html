⚡ Technology Overview

Multi-Factor Vehicle Immobilization & Physical-Presence Gating

Modern automotive keyless entry systems are vulnerable to wireless Relay Station Attacks (RSA), key cloning, and unauthorized drive-away theft. The Quackworks Acoustic Lock (QAL) solves keyless vulnerability by decoupling vehicle entry from engine start, establishing an independent Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) physical barrier:

FACTOR 1: ACCESS & INGRESS Existing primary wireless keys (RF, BLE, or UWB) handle exterior door unlocking and wake the vehicle systems.
FACTOR 2: PROPULSION INTERLOCK Powertrain circuits remain hard-isolated until an encrypted physical-presence token is verified inside the cabin.

💡 Zero Added Vehicle Hardware: Engineered as a software-defined security layer designed to integrate into existing vehicle architectures and factory microphone infrastructure.
