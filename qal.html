⚡ Executive Summary

Solving Keyless Theft at the Physical Layer (0 Added Vehicle BOM)

Modern automotive keyless entry systems are inherently vulnerable to wireless Relay Station Attacks (RSA), OBD-II port key cloning, and simultaneous device theft. The Quackworks Acoustic Lock (QAL) solves this vulnerability by introducing an independent, out-of-band acoustic physical-presence layer:

FACTOR 1: ACCESS & INGRESS Existing RF key fob, BLE digital key, or UWB transceiver unlocks exterior vehicle doors and wakes the system.
