Welcome to Duckmon: Collectable Card Multiverse

Duckmon began as a small minigame inside my TextZ Ombie project, but it quickly grew into something much bigger. Inspired by Triple Triad (Final Fantasy VIII) and classic Pokémon-style turn-based mechanics, Duckmon blends strategic grid battles, deep roguelite crawling, and squad RPG combat across an infinite multiverse of unique duck factions, lore, and rarity systems.

About the Game

Duckmon is a multiverse‑driven collectible card battler built around fast, tactical, grid‑based combat and rich RPG systems. The game features a full Story Mode spanning 30 stories across 10 chapters, each with unique art, choices, branching routes, and multiple endings.

Game Modes & Core Features

⚔️

5v5 Squad Battle (RPG Draft)

A turn-based RPG draft combat mode inspired by classic Pokémon battles. Build a 5-card squad where each card possesses 4 unique tactical skills for deep elemental and strategic counterplay.

🎲

RNG Spinner Roguelite Dungeon

A standalone spinner-driven dungeon crawler with 45+ procedural floors. Pick 1 of 310 starter cards and spin through random encounters, shops, bosses, portals, mystery events, and rare loot.

🎧

Music Theater & Visualizer

A dedicated in-game Music Theater featuring a dynamic audio visualizer and full soundtrack playlist to enjoy all original and featured multiverse themes.

🃏

310 Cards & Tactical 5×4 Grid

310 fully illustrated collectible cards spanning multiversal factions, backed by full Deckbuilder & Binder collection systems and Supabase cloud progression.
