Starting With Zero Experience: No Tutorials, Pure AI Collaboration

When I opened Unity for the very first time, I had zero coding experience, no game dev background, and zero formal training. Instead of getting lost in tutorial hell, I dove straight in and embraced artificial intelligence to help me bring my vision to life inside the engine.

"I am the architect. The AI is my expert."

AI does not just do everything on autopilot. I design the vision, direct the logic, instruct the workflow, and troubleshoot every mechanic. We collaborate as a team - turning complex ideas into working systems, one prompt, bug fix, and late night at a time.

From building this entire website with zero web experience to shipping DEV.to challenge entries and constructing full multimodal card systems in Duckmon - this is what happens when stubborn curiosity meets execution.
